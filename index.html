Background

About Me

I lead the Coordinated Hazard Assessment and Mapping Program (CHAMP) at the Illinois State Water Survey, University of Illinois - a FEMA Cooperating Technical Partner program with roughly $15M in active grants covering floodplain mapping, hydraulic analysis, and flood risk modernization across Illinois.

In my 50s, I started teaching myself to code. What started as workflow automation became something bigger: AI-powered tools that can do in hours what used to take weeks of manual engineering work. RAS Agent, FloodStack, and Risk Agent are the result.

I'm a co-chair of the ASFPM Mitigation & Engineering Standards Committee and serve on the CHAMP AI Workgroup. I believe responsible AI in engineering means augmenting expertise, not replacing judgment.

Open Source & Research

Projects

Tools I've built or contributed to - mostly aimed at automating the painful parts of hydraulic modeling, flood risk analysis, and FEMA compliance workflows.

RAS Agent
AI-powered HEC-RAS automation pipeline. Stages 1–10: watershed delineation through SPHERE loss estimation and cloud-native GeoParquet export. Cuts weeks of manual work to hours.
Python HEC-RAS FEMA FFRD GitHub ↗
🌊
FloodStack
Open-source orchestration layer chaining TRITON, RAS Agent, and SPHERE into a complete flood risk quantification pipeline. DepthGrid as common currency; cloud-native from the ground up.
Python GeoParquet DuckDB GitHub ↗

Risk Agent
Shifts flood risk from binary floodplain membership to continuous, probabilistic quantification - Expected Annual Damage per structure, hazard curves, and reach-level prioritization aligned with FEMA's FFRD initiative.
